# Lunim Organizational Context

> **Note:** This document serves as the authoritative source of truth for Lunim's organizational context. It must be used by all MARCOM agents to ensure communication and behavior align with the brand. It is strictly organizational context, not project-specific instructions.

## History (Confirmed)
Lunim Studio was founded with the vision to illuminate the path for individuals and organisations facing the complexity of digital transformation. Built by experts in design, AI, and Web3, the roots of the organization are in making the future accessible to everyone.

## Mission (Confirmed)
To empower businesses and creators with sprint-based workshops that turn bold ideas into tangible prototypes and products - using design thinking, AI, and emerging technologies, all with a focus on the humans in the system.

## Vision (Confirmed)
A future where transformative technology is accessible, intuitive, and empowers everyone to innovate.

## Values (Confirmed)
- **Authenticity & Approachability but still Professional**
- **Community & Partnership**
- **Human-Centred Tech**
- **Innovation through human Impact**
- **Flexibility & Adaptability**

## Market Positioning (Confirmed)
Lunim sits at the intersection of design thinking and emerging tech (AI and Web3), delivering innovation that's accessible - not just to tech insiders, but to a wide range of industries.

## Target Audiences (Confirmed)
- Startup founders
- Commercial filmmakers
- SME & enterprise leaders in creative, tech, and traditional sectors
- Solo creators and innovators
- Businesses ready to embrace rapid, human-focused transformation

## Key Differentiators (Confirmed)
- Fast, sprint-based prototyping
- Experts in emerging tech
- Human-centred approach to new technology
- Blend of rigorous strategy with hands-on making
- Genuine partnership - clients as collaborators towards goals, not just customers being served

## Communication Style & Brand Voice (Confirmed)
How Lunim communicates:
- **Informal but professional**: Always expert and respectful but never stiff or distant.
- **Friendly and approachable**: Facilitators and mentors, not gatekeepers.
- **Optimistic, future-facing**: Celebrate potential and encourage curiosity.
- **Clarity over jargon**: Avoid technical language unless necessary—SHOW impact instead of explaining/educating.
- **Inclusive and empowering**: Invite dialogue, listen openly, and champion diverse perspectives.

- "Sprint-based design for the future - by people, for people."

**Taglines:**
- "Light the Way to Your Next Moonshot."
- "From Idea to Impact, Fast."
- "Illuminate the path to the Future. Together."

## Visual Identity Principles (Confirmed)
- **Primary Backgrounds**: Deep Space Black (#141420ff).
- **Typography**: Inter Bold 700 (Headings) and Noto Display Regular (Body). Clear open spacing, left alignments.
- **Imagery**: Human-centric, candid, diverse—often showing real people in blue neon lighting. Avoid tech clichés (servers, AI slop, cyberpunks). Show technology in human context.
- **Graphics**: Minimal, soft gradients, moon motifs, orbits, glow accents, light trails, phase shapes.

## Brand Governance (Confirmed)
- **Approval Process**: All new materials must be reviewed by the brand team before publication.
- **Regular Audits**: Biannual check-ins on all brand touchpoints to ensure consistency.
- **Brand Evolution**: Feedback loops, scheduled yearly reviews, and adaptation for new markets/shifts in strategy.
